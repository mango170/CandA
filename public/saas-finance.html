SaaS & technology

Understand recurring revenue beyond the bank deposits.

Subscriptions, usage charges and one-off projects can tell different stories. Stripe receipts alone do not explain retention, earned revenue or the cash needed to reach the next milestone.

Revenue and retention

Build a documented bridge from billing activity to management metrics.

  • ARR and MRR movements with agreed definitions
  • Customer cohorts, churn and net revenue retention
  • Deferred revenue schedules and reconciliation to the ledger

Runway and growth

Connect commercial assumptions to the resources they require.

  • Driver-based hiring and operating forecasts
  • Channel economics and customer acquisition scenarios
  • Cash runway, funding needs and downside cases

Reporting foundations

Make the monthly pack repeatable.

  • Stripe-to-ledger reconciliation design
  • Budget-versus-actual reporting and investor schedules
  • Close responsibilities and a metric-definition register

Measures that support decisions

MRR, ARR, gross margin, churn, retention and runway. Customer acquisition payback is useful only when cost allocation and cohort data support it.

Practical questions

Is ARR the same as revenue?

No. ARR is a management measure of recurring contract value. Financial reporting revenue follows the applicable accounting policy; the two need a documented reconciliation.

Can you model a software launch?

Yes. A defined model can compare direct and affiliate sales, acquisition costs, staffing and funding needs. Assumptions remain visible and can be challenged.
