How we approach financial models.

A model should make its assumptions visible, connect its calculations and help people understand the effect of changing the plan. Whether we are building, reviewing or reconstructing it, we start with the decision it needs to support.

Trace the drivers

Revenue, costs, working capital and financing should connect to the way the business or project operates.

Test the relationships

Check the links between schedules, the balance sheet and cash flow, including what happens when a key assumption changes.

Separate the assumptions

Make inputs, calculations and outputs clear enough to review and update without obscuring the logic.

Explain the conclusions

Show the implications, the limitations and the questions that still need evidence. A well-presented output is useful only when the reasoning can be followed.
