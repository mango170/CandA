What you receive

We agree the model or reporting pack, assumptions, review dates and handover. We explain the results and unresolved questions. Lenders and investors make their own funding decisions; a forecast cannot guarantee an outcome.

Your questions, answered

Can you prepare a bank project report and CMA data?

Yes. We use the business plan, historical accounts, funding request and proposed terms to prepare the agreed projections and supporting schedules.

Can you prepare a valuation for a business decision?

Yes. We build valuation models for planning and transaction analysis. If a transaction requires a prescribed valuation report, we identify the need for a separately appointed eligible valuer.
