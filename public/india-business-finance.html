What to bring and what to expect

Bring recent financial information, debt schedules, the business plan and proposed terms. We review the assumptions, identify missing information and agree an analytical deliverable. Bank approval and funding decisions remain with the lender or investor.

Practical questions

Do you guarantee funding or loan sanctions?

No. Projections and readiness analysis help explain a proposal; they do not secure lender approval or investor commitments.

Is advisory valuation a prescribed valuation report?

No. Financial models and scenario analysis support decisions. Where a prescribed report is required, that is a separate professional appointment outside this advisory offer.
