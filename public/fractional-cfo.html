Home / Fractional CFO support

Ongoing financial perspective

A regular financial review.
A clearer next decision.

For business owners and organisation leaders who need forward-looking financial support alongside their accounting function.

When this is useful

Your accounts are being prepared, but you need a clearer view of cash, margins and the financial consequences of your plans.

What you receive

  • A management reporting pack with agreed KPIs and commentary.
  • A cash forecast and a view of upcoming funding or working capital needs.
  • Budget-versus-actual analysis, with the material differences explained.
  • A financial review meeting and a record of agreed next steps.

How the work progresses

  1. Understand the starting point

    Review available accounts, reporting routines, cash commitments and the questions management wants answered.

  2. Agree the reporting framework

    Choose the KPIs, cash horizon, reporting format and responsibilities that fit the business.

  3. Build the first useful view

    Prepare the initial dashboard and forecast, documenting data gaps and material assumptions.

  4. Review and establish the rhythm

    Walk through the findings and agree the reporting calendar and next priorities.

An illustrative first-month sequence. The starting point and data readiness determine the actual schedule.

The working rhythm

Usually structured around a monthly reporting and review cycle, with forecast updates and meeting frequency agreed in the scope.

What helps us start

  • Recent management accounts and balance sheet
  • Current budget, if available
  • Cash balances, receivables, payables and debt schedules
  • Your priorities and existing team responsibilities

Scope & fees

Scope, timing, responsibilities and fees are agreed before work starts.

Bookkeeping, tax filings and statutory assurance are scoped separately. CFO support can work alongside your existing accountant.

Practical questions

Before the engagement.

When is fractional CFO support useful?

It is useful when accounts are being prepared but leadership needs a clearer view of cash, performance, budgets and the financial implications of its plans. The work can sit alongside an existing accounting function.

Can you support board and investor reporting?

Yes. Management, board and investor reporting can be included in the scope, with the format, reporting cycle and inputs agreed for the organisation.
