Manufacturing & distribution

Understand the products using your margin and cash.

Production lead times, changing input costs and stock commitments make headline gross profit an incomplete view. Product economics and working-capital reporting need to work together.

Product profitability

Identify the drivers of margin movement.

  • SKU and product-family profitability analysis
  • Budget-versus-actual price and cost comparisons
  • Cost allocation and landed-cost documentation

Inventory and production planning

Translate operating assumptions into funding needs.

  • Demand, lead-time and safety-stock models
  • Stock ageing and purchasing scenarios
  • Supplier terms and production commitments in cash forecasts

Finance systems

Make monthly reporting repeatable.

  • Odoo accounting and inventory mapping reviews
  • Ledger-to-stock reconciliations and data exceptions
  • Management packs with documented close routines

Measures that support decisions

Product contribution, gross margin, stock days, receivable days, payable days and forecast cash conversion. Standard versus actual cost differences remain visible.

How the work starts

Bring product masters, stock movements, purchase terms and recent reporting. We check how quantities and costs reach the ledger, agree allocation rules and connect the product view to cash.

We agree the initial diagnostic, reconcile the starting information, develop the reporting or model, and walk through findings and update responsibilities. Ongoing support follows only where agreed.
