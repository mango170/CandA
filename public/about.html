Chartered Accountant · Strategic Finance

Abhishek Sachdeva, FCA

Abhishek brings more than 10 years of experience across accounting, audit, financial modeling, FP&A and advisory. His work includes investor and board reporting, venture-fund reporting, model reviews and acquisition analysis.

He has worked with business owners, CFOs, accounting firms, investors and nonprofit organisations across international markets. Earlier, as a Credit Manager at a private bank in India, he managed a portfolio of more than 50 SME borrowers.

His professional experience includes financial analysis and modeling with ERS Advisory, and venture-fund finance and accounting with NZVC.

His professional background also includes work as an authorised partner and consultant for Hobasa, an AI-based financial analysis platform.

He is the author of ABC of Finance: Journey to your Financial Freedom and co-author of How to prepare for Recession?

Qualifications & professional background

Fellow Chartered Accountant (FCA). ICAI certificate courses in Forensic Accounting and Fraud Detection (FAFD) and Concurrent Audit of Banks (CCA). B.Com and M.A. in English Literature.

Abhishek also serves as Fractional CFO & Accounting Advisor at Straight Talk CPAs and is a partner at A J A & Co.
