Tax returns backed by clear records.

We help startups, business owners, professionals, artists, consultants and companies prepare and file their Income Tax returns. We connect your books, tax records and supporting documents so the return reflects your income and the decisions behind it.

Registrations & returns

We organise the information needed to start and maintain your tax compliance.

TDS, TCS & ongoing compliance

We reconcile deductions and collections with your records.

  • TDS/TCS workings, returns and correction statements
  • Advance-tax calculations and payment planning
  • AIS, Form 26AS and ledger reconciliations

Planning, corrections & notices

What you receive

We provide the agreed tax computations, return copies, filing acknowledgements and a list of outstanding actions. You approve the final figures and complete the applicable verification or authorisation before submission.

Your questions, answered

Can you take over from my previous accountant?

Yes. We review filed returns, open demands, tax credits and available records, then agree the periods and tasks we will handle.

Can you help if my books and tax records do not match?

Yes. We compare the records, investigate differences and prepare reconciliations before finalising the return. If records are missing, we explain what still needs to be resolved.

Tell us what you need help with.

Share your business type, the registrations or filings involved and your timeline. We will explain the next steps and agree the work and fee before starting.
