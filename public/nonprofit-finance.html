CFO support, accounting projects and reporting for organisations balancing programme delivery, funding commitments and accountability to boards, donors and members.

Who this is for

When the finance work has outgrown the current setup.

You may run several funded programmes, report to different stakeholders, or manage subscriptions and events alongside day-to-day operations. The aim is a reliable view of what has been spent, what is committed and what remains available.

01 / Reporting & accountability

Funds, programmes & projects

Organise the accounts around the way your work is funded and delivered.

  • Fund, grant and project reporting structures
  • Budget-versus-actual reports and cost allocations
  • Schedules supporting donor and utilisation reporting
  • A clear record of assumptions and unresolved items
02 / Planning & oversight

Cash, budgets & board reporting

Give leadership a useful view of funding timing, commitments and operating capacity.

  • Programme and organisation-wide budgets
  • Cash forecasts that distinguish restricted funds from available operating cash
  • Board and finance-committee reporting packs
  • Scenario planning for delayed funding or changing costs
03 / Accounting foundations

Clean records & workable controls

Resolve the accounting issues that make reporting slow or difficult to trust.

  • Backlog cleanup and balance-sheet reconciliations
  • Chart of accounts, project codes and system setup
  • Close routines, documentation and audit preparation
  • Membership dues, event income and related reporting for associations

Practical questions

Before the engagement.

Can you support grant and programme reporting?

Yes. The agreed work can include fund and project reporting structures, budget comparisons, cost allocations and schedules supporting donor or utilisation reporting. The requirements follow your funding agreements and existing professional advisers.

Can you help an association with membership and event income?

Yes. Accounting and reporting can be organised around membership dues, event income, programme costs and the information needed by the board or finance committee.

Can we begin with a defined accounting project?

Yes. A backlog cleanup, difficult reconciliation, reporting setup or preparation of supporting schedules can be scoped as a separate project. Ongoing finance support can follow where useful.
