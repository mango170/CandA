Energy, EV & infrastructure

Connect project activity to investment economics.

Utilisation, tariffs, investment timing and commercial agreements interact over a long horizon. A useful model makes those relationships visible instead of hiding the result inside a headline return.

Project economics

Build the operating and financial drivers together.

  • Volume, utilisation and pricing assumptions
  • Capex, operating costs and revenue-sharing logic
  • Funding, repayment and tax assumptions agreed to scope

Scenario analysis

Test the commercial plan before committing.

  • NPV, IRR and payback comparisons
  • Utilisation, cost and tariff sensitivities
  • Downside funding and debt-service scenarios

Review and handover

Make the model usable by the decision team.

  • Formula, sign and scenario consistency checks
  • Assumption logs and supporting schedules
  • Model walkthrough and update instructions

Measures that support decisions

Utilisation, contribution per operating unit, project and equity IRR, NPV and debt-service coverage. IRR is considered alongside cash timing and downside funding, rather than on its own.

How the work starts

Bring the operating plan, investment schedule, draft commercial terms and finance assumptions. We map the value drivers, build a traceable model and test the scenarios the decision requires.

We agree the initial diagnostic, reconcile the starting information, develop the reporting or model, and walk through findings and update responsibilities. Ongoing support follows only where agreed.

Practical questions

Is this an engineering feasibility study?

No. Financial analysis uses technical inputs supplied by the project team and does not establish engineering performance or regulatory approval.

Can you review an existing project model?

Yes. The existing specialist model-review service checks formulas, structure, assumptions and scenario logic. These technical checks are distinct from statutory assurance.
