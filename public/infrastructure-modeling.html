Connect capital investment, operating assumptions, commercial terms and cash flow in a model built around the project.

When this is useful

For infrastructure operators, project developers, investors and finance professionals evaluating a commercial proposal, pricing structure, capital investment or operating scenario.

What the work can include

  • A model linking project drivers to revenue, operating costs, capital expenditure and cash flow.
  • Pricing and commercial-term analysis, including revenue sharing where relevant.
  • Investment appraisal using NPV and IRR within the agreed scope.
  • Scenario and sensitivity analysis around demand, utilisation, costs and timing.
  • Documented assumptions and a walkthrough of the model’s conclusions.

Relevant experience

Professional experience includes EV charging in the UAE, Australian gas pipeline and energy infrastructure models, biomethane NPV and IRR analysis, and port and maritime projects. These engagements have involved pricing, investment analysis and scenario comparisons.

Practical questions

Before the engagement.

Which infrastructure sectors are relevant to your experience?

The professional experience behind C&A includes EV charging, energy infrastructure, gas pipelines, biomethane, ports and maritime projects. The scope of a new engagement follows the project’s own commercial and operating structure.

Can the model compare different commercial terms?

Yes. Pricing, revenue sharing, utilisation, operating costs and investment timing can be incorporated as explicit assumptions and compared through scenarios.

Do NPV and IRR replace commercial due diligence?

No. They are model outputs based on assumptions. The model should be considered alongside the commercial evidence, technical assessments and advice relevant to the project.
