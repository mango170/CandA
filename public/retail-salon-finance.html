Know what each location contributes.

Location reporting

Make performance comparable.

  • Location P&Ls with a consistent account structure
  • POS, processor and bank reconciliation workflows
  • Discount, refund and service-versus-product income views

Labour and operating scenarios

Evaluate costs alongside revenue assumptions.

  • Payroll and occupancy ratio analysis
  • Break-even and location expansion scenarios
  • Shared overhead allocation with transparent assumptions

Cash and management packs

Bring the group into one view.

  • Consolidated and location-level budget comparisons
  • Short-term cash forecasts and commitment schedules
  • Monthly exception lists for owner review

Measures that support decisions

Location contribution, average ticket, payroll-to-sales, occupancy cost and break-even revenue. Labour analysis is financial decision support, with management retaining employment decisions.
