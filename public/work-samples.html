Advisory deliverables

Illustrative formats · Fictional examples

These examples show how findings and decisions can be organised within an engagement. They are demonstration formats, not records of completed client work.

Model-review findings

Issue
Receivable days are held constant while the downside scenario assumes slower customer payments.
Implication
The downside case understates working capital and the funding requirement.
Recommended correction
Link collection timing to the scenario assumptions and reconcile the resulting cash movement.
Review evidence
Compare the revised cash forecast with the receivables schedule and document the changed assumption.

Accounting-cleanup findings

Account
Payment processor clearing.
Issue
Net settlements have been recorded as sales, leaving fees and timing differences unexplained.
Supporting work
Reconcile gross sales, refunds, fees and settlement timing to the ledger and bank.
Handover
Adjustment schedule, unresolved items and a repeatable monthly reconciliation.

Board reporting pack

Performance
Actual versus budget, with commentary on the material movements.
Cash & commitments
Cash forecast, funding requirements and the timing of major payments.
Scenarios
Financial implications of a delayed launch or slower collections.
Decisions
Questions for the board, agreed actions and the next reporting date.
