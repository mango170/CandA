What to bring and what to expect

Bring the jurisdictions involved, entity or residency facts, transaction purpose and relevant agreements. We first identify the information gaps and the questions needing specialist advice. Eligibility, approvals and regulatory outcomes are not assumed.

Practical questions

Does this include remittance approval or representation?

No. This offer covers consultation, financial analysis and documentation readiness. Approval, filing and representation require separately confirmed authority and appointments.

Can you promise eligibility for charitable registrations?

No. Readiness discussions help organise information. Eligibility and acceptance depend on the facts and the relevant authority’s requirements.
