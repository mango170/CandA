Your questions, answered

Can you help an Indian consultant with overseas clients?

Yes. We can connect income records, foreign receipts, withholding and Indian tax filings. For export-related registrations and documentation, see our import-export and GST services.

Does financial analysis itself approve an overseas transaction?

No. Where a bank or regulator must approve a transaction, that decision follows its own process. We help organise the records and financial analysis required for your next step.
