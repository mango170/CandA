Measures that support decisions

How the work starts

Start with a contract summary, cost reports, billing schedules and a list of open variations. We reconcile project data to the ledger, identify missing commitments and build a forecast with documented estimates.

We agree the initial diagnostic, reconcile the starting information, develop the reporting or model, and walk through findings and update responsibilities. Ongoing support follows only where agreed.

The scope is built around your records and financial questions. Sector examples describe reporting needs, rather than a claim of a named C&A client engagement.

Practical questions

Can you forecast jobs with incomplete cost data?

We can show known costs, estimates and gaps separately. An estimate is not treated as a verified cost, and management must validate completion assumptions.

Do you provide engineering or contract opinions?

The work concerns finance, accounting and financial models. Technical certification and legal contract interpretation require the appropriate advisers.
