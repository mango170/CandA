Support through the life of your company or LLP.

We help founders and management teams organise registrations, annual filings and changes without losing track of the records behind them. We bring the accounting and compliance tasks into one practical calendar.

Maintain & update

  • Annual company and LLP return preparation and filing support
  • ROC/MCA event-based filings for agreed business changes

Close with a clear plan

We review the position before preparing closure applications.

  • Company strike-off and LLP closure application support

  • Tracking application queries and final status

Documents and signing

We prepare the agreed forms, attachments and filing tracker. Directors or designated partners provide the required approvals and signatures. If an MCA form requires a practising professional’s declaration or signature, we identify that requirement before work starts so you can arrange the separate appointment.

Your questions, answered

Can you handle registrations and ongoing ROC filings?

Yes. We support incorporation applications, annual filings and agreed changes, with a document checklist and filing calendar for your entity.

Can a business close immediately after it stops trading?

We first review the entity’s status, pending returns, liabilities and closure conditions. We explain the steps and prepare the appropriate application once the requirements are met. Approval rests with the Registrar.

Tell us what you need help with.

Share your business type, the registrations or filings involved and your timeline. We will explain the next steps and agree the work and fee before starting.
