Your information

A straightforward inquiry process.

This notice explains the information collected through this website.

Information you submit

The inquiry form collects your name, email, business or organisation details, requested service, timing and message. C&A Advisory Services uses this information to review and respond to your request and discuss a potential engagement.

Website hosting and form processing

This website is hosted by Netlify. When you submit an inquiry, Netlify processes the form submission and stores it for review through the website administration account. Email notifications may also be configured. Netlify may process technical information and connection logs to operate the service and prevent abuse. Access to inquiries is limited to supporting your request and engagement.

Spam protection

The form includes a hidden field to help detect automated submissions. Netlify may apply additional spam filtering.

Calculator inputs

The cash-runway calculator runs in your browser. Its input amounts are not submitted through the inquiry form.

Access and removal

To request access, correction or deletion of an inquiry, contact abhisheksachdeva.ca@gmail.com. Please include the approximate submission date and the email address used.

External services

Netlify, Calendly, Amazon, Upwork, Fiverr and other linked services handle information under their own policies. See Netlify’s privacy policy for information about the hosting and form provider.

Keep the first message simple

Please share a business summary and the question you want to discuss. Do not send passwords, payment details or confidential client records through this form.
