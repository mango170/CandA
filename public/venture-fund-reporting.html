When this is useful

For venture funds, investment businesses and their finance professionals who need specialist capacity for accounting work, financial reporting or a defined reporting project.

What the work can include

  • Accounting support and reconciliations for the agreed records and periods.

  • A documented handover of completed work, open questions and next steps.

Practical questions

Before the engagement.

Can you work alongside our existing finance professionals?

Yes. A defined engagement can support an internal team, accounting firm or existing fund advisers. Information, deliverables and review points are agreed at the outset.

Is the work limited to ongoing engagements?

No. A reporting requirement, reconciliation exercise or accounting project can be scoped separately. Recurring support can follow if useful.

Does this include regulated fund administration or an audit opinion?

The offering is accounting and reporting support. Regulated administration, legal or tax opinions and statutory audit work require their own arrangements and professional sign-off.
