Financial transaction support

Understand the numbers
behind the business.

A focused review of the available financial information to support a buyer’s acquisition decision.

When this is useful

You are evaluating an acquisition and want to understand earnings, cash generation, working capital and the questions to resolve before proceeding.

What you receive

  • Historical financial performance analysis and identification of material movements.
  • A review of reported earnings and proposed adjustments, supported by the available evidence.
  • Cash flow and working capital analysis within the agreed scope.
  • A concise findings report, open questions and a discussion of their implications for your decision.

How the work progresses

  1. Scope the review

    Discuss the proposed transaction, available records and priority concerns.

  2. Review the evidence

    Compare the financial records and supporting information provided.

  3. Resolve the questions

    Identify gaps, follow up on explanations and distinguish confirmed findings from unresolved items.

  4. Discuss the implications

    Present the findings and financial questions to consider with your wider transaction team.

Timing depends on access to records, the scope and seller responses. Agree the review timetable before starting.

The working rhythm

A project engagement aligned with transaction milestones and the availability of the seller’s information.

What helps us start

  • Historical financial statements and management accounts
  • Relevant bank, receivables, payables and payroll records
  • Proposed price, structure and deal assumptions
  • Supporting schedules and access to financial queries

Scope & fees

Scope, timing, responsibilities and fees are agreed before work starts.

This is a scoped financial review. Legal, tax, regulatory and formal audit or assurance work require their own scope and advisers.

Practical questions

Before the engagement.

What does an acquisition financial review cover?

The agreed scope can cover historical performance, reported earnings, working capital, cash flow and financial questions arising from the available records.

Can you coordinate with other advisers?

Yes. Financial work can be discussed alongside the buyer’s existing accounting, tax and legal advisers, with responsibilities agreed for the transaction.
