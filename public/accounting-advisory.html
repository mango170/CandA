Complex accounting projects

Resolve the difficult balances.
Build accounts you can use.

Experienced accounting support for complicated cleanups, new setups, system transitions and reporting issues that need careful judgment.

When this is useful

Your accounts contain unresolved balances, the reporting does not reconcile, a new system needs a reliable starting point, or a complex transaction needs a clear accounting approach.

What you receive

  • A diagnostic of the accounting issues, records available and priorities.
  • Reconciliations and documented corrections for the agreed accounts and periods.
  • Chart of accounts, opening balances and reporting structures where setup is needed.
  • Accounting treatment recommendations, supporting schedules and a record of unresolved questions.
  • A practical handover with close routines, documentation and responsibilities.

How the work progresses

  1. Diagnose and prioritise

    Review the records, identify the difficult areas and agree what must be resolved before the reporting deadline.

  2. Reconcile and resolve

    Trace balances to supporting information, investigate differences and document the agreed corrections.

  3. Set up the reporting foundation

    Align the ledger, opening balances, account mapping and reporting structure with how the organisation operates.

  4. Explain and hand over

    Walk through the changes, remaining limitations and the routines needed to keep the records reliable.

The timetable follows the volume of records, age of the backlog, complexity and access to supporting information.

The working rhythm

A defined project with milestones and review points. Ongoing close review or CFO support can follow where useful.

What helps us start

  • Trial balance and general ledger for the relevant periods
  • Previous financial statements and reconciliation schedules
  • Details of the systems, accounting issues and reporting deadline
  • Supporting records for the balances or transactions being reviewed

Scope & fees

Scope, timing, responsibilities and fees are agreed before work starts.

The scope can cover businesses, NGOs, nonprofits and associations. Any formal audit, tax filing or jurisdiction-specific sign-off is agreed separately.

Practical questions

Before the engagement.

Can you help with complicated historical accounts?

Yes. The work can cover difficult reconciliations, unresolved balances, historical errors and supporting schedules. The starting point is the records available and the reporting requirement.

Can you set up an accounting system?

Yes. A setup can include the chart of accounts, opening balances, account mapping, reporting structures and practical close routines within an agreed scope.
